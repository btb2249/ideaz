Privacy Policy — IDEAZ

Last updated: November 23, 2026

IDEAZ is made by Bethel Ventures. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

The short version: nothing leaves your phone. IDEAZ has no servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. We do not collect, sell, share, or send your personal information anywhere.

How the app works

  • IDEAZ is an idea marketplace that runs entirely on your phone. The listings you create, the ideas you browse, and the sales recorded in the app all live on your device only.
  • Purchases are test-mode only. Buying an idea in this version is a sandbox test purchase: no real money moves, no card is charged, and no payment information is collected, asked for, or stored — ever. The buy flow only walks through how a real sale will work.
  • IDEAZ takes a flat 10% commission in the sale math shown in the app; in test mode that math is shown for illustration and no money actually changes hands.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your listings and the app's sales ledger are stored in a private database on your phone only.
  • This information is never uploaded, emailed, or transmitted anywhere. There is no server for it to go to.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also delete any listing from inside the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not take real payments and does not ask for bank, card, or account information.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or messages.
  • It does not show ads and contains no advertising or tracking software.

Children: IDEAZ is made for adults. Buying and selling ideas are business matters for people 18 and older.

Changes: If a future version changes how the app handles information — including any version where real purchases are switched on — this policy will be updated and the new version posted at the same address before the change takes effect.
